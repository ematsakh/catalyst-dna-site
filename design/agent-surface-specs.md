# Agent Work-Surface Specs · Phase A · v1.0

One paragraph per surface. Each names the structural idea, the information architecture, the trust-layer placements, and the engagement-mode boundary — enough for a Phase B chunk to build the page without re-deriving intent. Palette, type, and motion come from the tokens file and trust layer; nothing below specifies any of the three. Locked rulings applied: CFO is Call Report Prep only; CHRO is interview-kit-only in public with resume assessment described-not-exposed; deposit attrition seats with the Chief Banking Officer. Forbidden lexicon (per playbook) is honored in every fixed string.

---

**1 · CEO Morning Brief / BOSS — executive signal stack (CEO/President · live).** A single dated column of at most seven signal cards under one lead-number masthead, readable top to bottom in four minutes. Each card is a fixed four-cell grid — *what changed / why this bank / suggested action / confidence* — with lane tag (bank-news, market, regulatory, worth-5-minutes), `.tl-asof` per card, `.tl-cite--live` on every claim, and `.tl-conf` in the fourth cell. Quiet lanes render as one-line "nothing recent" rows, never padded. A dashed `.tl-engage` block at the top of the stack names the overnight-internal-numbers lane (deposit flows, pipeline, liquidity) as engagement mode — the production BOSS surface's roll-up slot, stated, not simulated. No search bar anywhere; the surface is a memo, not a query box. Phase B must remap boss_demo.jsx onto the token palette before this surface absorbs the ceobrief route.

**2 · Banker Briefing — relationship canvas (Chief Lending Officer · live).** The subject sits in a centered header block (name, meeting context, verified/unverified state); around it, five canvas blocks in a 2-column flow: snapshot, developments (each with `.tl-asof` and `.tl-cite--live`), likely-needs hypotheses (each tied to its public signal), risks, questions-to-ask. Exactly three talking-point cards run full-width above the fold, each opener carrying its evidence chips inline — the "smart opener with receipts" is the surface's signature. A `.tl-engage` strip below the header names the relationship-side checks (existing exposure, statement staleness, KYC gaps, servicing history) that production runs from core + CRM. Prints to one page; a 90-second read order is marked by block numbering, not motion.

**3 · Deposit Attrition — retention radar (Chief Banking Officer · fictional packet).** One flagged relationship rendered as a brief, not a dashboard: signal header (flag id, rule, priority, `.tl-asof`), a 12-month balance strip with the run-rate and six-month projection shown as `.tl-cite--derived` glass-box calcs, then the surface's centerpiece — a retention-economics panel where replacement cost (wholesale spread × balance at risk × duration), retention cost, and break-even rate each display their formula and editable-input framing. Drivers render as hypothesis rows, each pinned to its specific flow signal with `.tl-conf`. The benign-pattern check (tax/payroll/seasonal) renders its verdict and basis explicitly. Sequenced play steps and the no-rate outreach note close above a `.tl-gate` ("pricing exceptions per [LP-6.1]" — RM holds the decision). `.tl-engage` names book-wide baseline validation, fairness screening, and holdout-lift measurement.

**4 · Call Report Prep — schedule close board (CFO · fictional packet).** A control-room board, not prose: a check-summary header (run/passed/flagged counts), then a tie-out table where every cross-schedule check shows its arithmetic as a `.tl-cite--derived` calc and a pass/fail state — the RC vs RC-C gap is the board's anchor finding. Flags render as `.tl-exc` rows (check failed / magnitude / what resolves / owner), each expanding to its examiner-ready draft explanation. A filing checklist with open/resolved states closes the board above a `.tl-gate` (controller holds filing). `.tl-engage` names GL-to-schedule reconciliation, prior-period tolerance history, and four-eyes certification. Board-narrative generation is out of scope for this surface by ruling — no narrative pane exists or is hinted.

**5 · Credit Memo — underwriting argument map (Chief Credit Officer · fictional packet).** The memo renders as a visible argument chain: claim nodes stack vertically, each expanding to evidence (with `.tl-cite--corpus` packet chips and derived calcs), risks, mitigants, and policy touchpoints citing [LP-x.x] with complies/exception state. Global DSCR and its sensitivity render arithmetic-first. A stress-lens section — visually distinct, structurally parallel — lists the two or three facts that most weaken the credit and the stressed coverage calc; it is a second reading of the same map, not a toggle that rewrites it. Missing documents surface as `.tl-exc` rows, never silent gaps. Draft recommendation and conditions precedent close above a `.tl-gate` ("Draft for analyst review — not a credit decision"). `.tl-engage` names K-1 tracing and covenant-conflict scans against the live book.

**6 · AML Triage + SAR Drafter — evidence chain (CRO/BSA Officer · fictional packet).** Three structurally separated bands that never blend: an evidence ledger (numbered facts E-1…E-n, each a packet citation), an analysis/hypothesis band (patterns with arithmetic shown, typologies with `.tl-conf`, every row referencing E-numbers), and the draft SAR narrative where each sentence carries its E-references — sentence-to-evidence traceability is the surface's signature. A five-pillar completeness meter (who/what/when/where/why + amounts) gates the disposition: incomplete pillars block the "ready" state. The missed-CTR remediation renders as its own flagged band. `.tl-gate` is the strongest on any surface: filing decisions rest with the BSA Officer, restated verbatim from the route closer. `.tl-engage` names prior-SAR similarity and sanctions/adverse-media corroboration.

**7 · Regulatory Change Monitor — obligation heatline (Chief Compliance Officer · live).** A profile bar (asset size, regulator, business lines) pins the top; below it, applies-to-you items render as three-column cards — *what changed / so what / now what* — each with agency+date, effective date, suggested owner, `.tl-cite--live` to primary source, and `.tl-conf`. A visually quieter does-not-reach-you band states the threshold or scope reason per item — negative findings are product behavior, not absence. Watch-list items carry horizon tags. A quiet period renders as a stated finding. `.tl-engage` names policy/control mapping completeness and guidance-page-edit detection (the continuous-feed check the demo's scan-window can't run). Printable committee-digest layout is the default, not an export mode.

**8 · Document Workflow — ops conveyor (COO · fictional packet).** The batch renders as a conveyor of document cards in received order; each card shows classification, source channel, then a per-field record grid — field / value / status (extracted · illegible · absent) / `.tl-conf` — where illegible and absent values render as literal states, never guessed values. Banking validators (insurance-cert loss-payee/expiry, payoff authorization/good-through) render pass/fail per card. The exception ledger is the surface's spine: every illegible field, absent artifact, and failed validator becomes a `.tl-exc` row cross-referenced to its document, with resolution and owner. Routing renders per card with hold conditions. `.tl-gate`: operations review holds resolution. `.tl-engage` names packet-history consistency and reviewer-authority checks.

**9 · System Contract Analyzer — contract x-ray (CIO · fictional packet).** An obligation ledger grouped by risk category — cost, exit, renewal, liability, security, data — where each extracted term carries its packet citation and a present/absent/adverse finding state; the absent data-use clause renders as a first-class *absent* finding, visually equal to adverse terms. A timeline band computes the notice deadline and days-remaining as derived calcs and flags inside-window state. Watchpoints (uncapped escalator compounded, proprietary-format de-conversion, SLA cap) render with their arithmetic. Negotiation asks render sequenced by leverage with the timing that creates it. The TCO-inputs block restates extracted numbers as calculator-ready fields — the surface's handoff to the FinAI TCO calculator. `.tl-gate`: counsel review. `.tl-engage` names standards-library clause comparison.

**10 · Vendor Security Review — control confidence ladder (CISO · fictional packet).** The ladder is the organizing structure: per control domain, a rung showing the *basis* of the bank's knowledge — self-attested / verified evidence / external telemetry / none — replacing any single-score badge by design. Below the ladder: the SOC 2 band (period, opinion, each exception with operational meaning and compensating evidence to request, staleness state, subservice gaps), the pen-test band (the unretested high finding rendered as the core `.tl-exc`, open mediums with targets), and the DPA band (adequate/missing per term). Follow-up questions render numbered by risk closed. The residual-risk statement — what signing today accepts — closes in plain prose above a `.tl-gate` (risk acceptance rests with the CISO). `.tl-engage` names questionnaire-vs-evidence reconciliation and fourth-party concentration.

**11 · Architecture Assessment — dependency atlas (CTO · fictional packet).** Opens with the posture summary and single most consequential finding, then a pillar grid (five well-architected pillars + integration debt + AI-readiness) where every finding names the inventory element it ties to — no free-floating judgments. A dependency band renders the point-to-point topology (from/to/kind/risk) with the single-FTE ETL and re-keying exposures called out. Assessment gaps — where the description was silent — render as stated gaps, never assumed answers. Recommendations render in dependency order, each stating what it unlocks and why it precedes the next; a wish-list ordering is a build error. `.tl-gate`: architecture decisions rest with the bank. `.tl-engage` names CMDB reconciliation and vendor-lifecycle EOL verification. Footer line preserved: this is the first hour of a DNA Blueprint.

**12 · Initiative Assessment — investment jury (Chief Transformation Officer · fictional packet).** Each assessment dimension — payback, benefits credibility, competitiveness, delivery risk, readiness, strategic fit — renders as an independent jury panel with a supports/neutral/against state, its finding, and any calc; non-financial panels carry equal visual weight to financial ones by construction. The sensitivity band shows the charter case and the realistic-ramp case side by side with arithmetic, plus the top assumption drivers and their swing. The verdict band renders proceed / proceed-with-conditions / defer with conditions as binding rows, not prose caveats. `.tl-gate`: investment decisions rest with the committee. `.tl-engage` names peer efficiency-ratio benchmarking and change-fatigue overlap against the initiative portfolio.

**13 · Interview Kit — structured interviewer cockpit (CHRO · live).** Role framing pins the top; competency panels follow, each holding its behavioral questions with a one-line "strong answer contains" anchor; the skills matrix, work sample (with time budget), and behaviorally-anchored scoring guide (strong/adequate/concern per area) complete the cockpit; the do-not-ask list renders in the trust layer's caution styling. The surface accepts a role and level only — by ruling, no candidate or resume input exists anywhere on the page, and the engagement-mode resume assessment (line-cited strengths/gaps, bias-guard, adverse-impact monitoring) appears solely as a described capability inside a `.tl-engage` block: named as a deliberate boundary, not a missing feature. `.tl-gate`: hiring decisions rest with humans, verbatim from the route closer.

**14 · Policy Q&A — policy lane (CDO · layer zero · fictional corpus).** A single question lane over the Cedarline corpus: the answer renders first, then its receipts — each cited section as a `.tl-cite--corpus` chip expanding to the verbatim clause with version state — and a coverage indicator (covered / partial / not-covered). The not-covered path is the surface's proof moment and renders as product behavior: the one-sentence statement, the nearest related section if one exists, and the escalation state — never an apologetic error. The conflicts band is present and typed but empty in the demo, labeled for the engagement-mode conflict detector. `.tl-gate`: operational decisions made on answers rest with the policy owner. `.tl-engage` names permissions-aware retrieval, corpus-wide conflict detection, and decision-use logging. As layer zero, this surface's chips set the citation grammar every other surface reuses.

---

**Cross-surface laws (restated for Phase B):** structure and information architecture are the only permitted axes of variation — palette, type, and motion come from the tokens file and trust layer everywhere, including BOSS after its Phase B remap. Every fictional-packet surface badges its universe honestly per the hub's live/fictional split. Every playbook cross-check the packet cannot support appears as a named `.tl-engage` item — simulated checks are a ship-blocker. Single-file pages and sample mode are preserved. The Cedarline/NorthArc universe stays internally consistent: Tillman Fabrication, Lakemont Vending, Green Mountain Orthopedics, and the NorthArc vendor recur across surfaces exactly as the Worker packets define them.
