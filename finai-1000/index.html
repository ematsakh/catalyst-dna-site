Methodology

How the map is built

The FinAI 1000 is a curated dataset, not a scrape. Every entry is added by hand from primary sources — funding announcements, product launches, regulatory filings, and sector newsletters — and carries its category, segment, geography, funding stage, and tier classification.

What qualifies

Companies whose product applies AI to a financial-services workflow — from fraud and compliance to lending, payments, core platforms, and capital markets. Both AI-native firms and AI-enabled platforms are tracked, and labeled as such.

Incumbent vs. challenger

Tier is assigned on ownership and scale, not market presence. Subsidiaries of major institutions and at-scale public companies are incumbents; venture-backed independents are challengers regardless of brand recognition.

What each entry carries

Name, domain, headquarters, functional categories, customer segments, funding stage and amount, notable investors, recent developments, and a one-line description of what the product actually does.

Update cadence

The dataset is refreshed continuously and republished monthly. Corrections and suggested additions are welcome — every submission is verified against primary sources before inclusion.
