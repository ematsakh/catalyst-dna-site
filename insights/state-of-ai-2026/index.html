Methodology

How this report is built

No independent survey of community-bank AI adoption exists, and this report does not pretend otherwise. It is built on a four-layer composite, each layer checking the others:

L1

Meta-study spine

A structured synthesis of published research on AI in banking — consulting studies, regulator publications, and academic work — normalised to the $1B–$50B segment and cited in full in the sources section.

L2

Outside-in, FDIC-anchored scoring

Public-record scoring across the community and regional bank population, anchored to FDIC and FFIEC institution data: bank websites, job postings, press releases, and 10-K filings, scored against a consistent rubric — the same five-dimension rubric that powers the Catalyst-DNA AI Readiness Self-Assessment.

L3

Operator deployment evidence

What production deployments actually cost, how long they take, and where they fail — drawn from Catalyst-DNA build work and the operating record of the team, including experience building one of the world's leading bank analytics functions.

L4

Operator panel

Structured conversations with TK n [target 15–25] executives and practitioners at community and regional banks — not a statistical sample, and not presented as one. The panel exists to pressure-test what the outside-in data cannot see.
